About This Directory

What This Coverage Directory Covers

One page, the whole city: where we operate, how fast we arrive, and how to add your address if it is missing.

This directory is the complete coverage map for Lahore Companions, and it exists to answer the only question that matters before you book: can you reach me? Lahore is not one city but a dozen layered on top of one another, and the difference between a twenty-minute arrival and a forty-five minute one is decided long before a companion leaves. Here we list the neighbourhoods we serve every night, grouped by the part of the city you are actually standing in, so you can find your own street in seconds.

Coverage works on a simple, honest model. Send your address, date, time and occasion to 0371-1399964 or tap the WhatsApp button on this page, and the concierge confirms your zone before anything else is discussed. Central areas such as Gulberg, MM Alam Road, DHA, Cantonment, Johar Town and Model Town usually see a companion within twenty to thirty minutes. Gated phases, outer societies and the villages on the city edge are covered on exactly the same terms, with travel time quoted honestly up front rather than promised away.

Every listing below is a real service area, not a keyword. Where a dedicated area guide exists, the name links straight through to it: Gulberg, DHA, Bahria Town, Johar Town, Model Town and the rest, each with its own venue notes, dispatch times and hotel protocol. Every individual entry tells you two things in a line or two: what that area is like, and how a meeting happens there.

If your street, sector, housing scheme or hotel is not on the list, it is still covered. Lahore has hundreds of named societies and we add new ones to the roster weekly, so a quick message with your landmark, gate number and timing is enough for the desk to confirm coverage and give you a realistic arrival window. One number, day or night: 0371-1399964. Cash on arrival, no deposit, no advance, the same terms in every postcode in this directory.

Decision Guide

How to Choose Your Base Area

Match the way you travel to the part of Lahore you should book in. The right base saves twenty minutes of waiting.

The nine regions above tell you what exists across the city. This section tells you which one to actually pick. Your base is the biggest single lever on how an evening runs. It decides arrival time, decides which restaurants are within reach, and decides how flexible you stay if the plan changes at the last minute. Since 2019 we have watched bookings sort themselves into five clear profiles, and each one has an obvious home address.

First visit, one or two nights

If this is your first time arranging a companion in Lahore, stay central. Gulberg, MM Alam Road and the Mall Road corridor give you the shortest dispatch, the most neutral meeting points and the easiest exit if you want to change direction. Hotel guests should almost always book to the room rather than a residence. Lobbies are designed for exactly this kind of arrival, and staff have seen it a hundred times. Our Lahore tourist guide covers timings, local etiquette and what to send first. First-timers tend to optimise on distance and under-optimise on comfort. Comfort wins every time.

Business traveller with a tight diary

Pick the hotel you are already staying in and let the companion come to you. Outcalls to Pearl Continental, Serena, Avari, Nishat and Four Points are routine for us, and the desk briefs the arrival on the specific lobby, parking and guest protocol before dispatch. If your daytime meetings sit in Gulberg and the evening ones sit near Emporium, base on Main Boulevard rather than the Mall. The cross-town drive at six in the evening will otherwise eat an hour you do not have. Send the property name with your brief, not just the area.

A late night out

Clubs and lounges cluster in Gulberg, along MM Alam and around Emporium Mall in Johar Town. Those three pockets cover almost every late venue in Lahore, and all three sit inside our fastest dispatch band. Ask for the meeting to happen at a fixed hour rather than having a companion wait around, and keep the address a public venue until you are ready to move on. Our night-club companions page goes deeper on venue timings, door etiquette and what to expect from staff. Everyone you meet through us is 18 or over, without exception.

The whole weekend, unhurried

With a full evening free, distance stops being a constraint. Bahria Town, Raiwind Road, DHA Phase 7 and Lake City all open up, and a dinner in Gulberg plus a slow drive south makes an easy four-hour plan. Weekend demand runs heavier than weekdays, so send your brief in the afternoon instead of ten at night. Clients who book Friday and Saturday early consistently tell us they get better matches. Weekend and full-evening bookings run on the same cash-on-arrival terms as any other night of the week.

The client who needs to be unremarked

Model Town, Garden Town, Askari Villas, Wapda Town and the cooperative societies off the canal suit this profile best. Streets are quiet, gates are polite, and an arriving car earns no second look. If you live in a family area, say so in your brief so the concierge selects someone who arrives without ceremony and leaves the same way. Here discretion beats speed, and the two are rarely in tension once the desk knows what matters to you.

Two honest caveats before you decide. First, avoid changing base across peak traffic, roughly eight to ten in the morning and five to eight in the evening. A twenty-minute trip becomes fifty, and no directory can argue with that. Second, outer societies along Bedian Road, Raiwind Road and Multan Road need a little notice. The terms stay identical and the price does not move, but the drive is real. Send your postcode and your plan to 0371-1399964, and the desk will tell you which base fits.
