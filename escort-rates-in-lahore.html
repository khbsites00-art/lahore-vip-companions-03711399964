Pricing Factors

What Affects Escort Rates in Lahore

Understanding the variables helps you choose wisely and know exactly what you're paying for.

⏱

Duration of Booking

Longer bookings offer better per-hour value. A 2-hour booking is the standard minimum. Full-night bookings (8 hours) provide the best hourly rate across all tiers.

Example: Budget 1hr = PKR 25K (25K/hr) vs Budget Full Night = PKR 65K (8.1K/hr) — 68% savings per hour.
👑

Companion Tier

Each tier reflects the companion's training, grooming, education, and exclusivity. Higher tiers offer superior conversational skills, styling, and social versatility.

Example: Budget companions are great for casual meets. VIP companions excel at black-tie events and high-profile dinners.
📍

Location & Travel

All bookings within Lahore city limits have zero travel surcharge. Out-of-city bookings (Islamabad, Multan, Faisalabad) incur PKR 15K–30K travel fees.

Example: Booking in Gulberg = no extra fee. Booking in Islamabad = PKR 20K travel surcharge added.
🎉

Occasion & Event Type

Casual dinner dates cost less than formal event accompaniment. Weddings, galas, and corporate events may require enhanced companionship with higher rates.

Example: Casual dinner = standard rate. Corporate gala accompaniment = +PKR 10K–20K depending on formality.
📅

Day of Week & Season

Weekend demand (Friday–Saturday) may increase rates by 10–15%. Holiday seasons like Eid and Valentine's Day see peak demand — book early to lock standard rates.

Example: Weekday booking = standard PKR 55K. Weekend = PKR 60K–63K for the same tier and duration.
🏨

Hotel Coordination

If you book your own hotel, no extra charge. If you need us to arrange hotel coordination, a fee of PKR 5K–15K applies based on hotel star rating.

Example: 3-star hotel coordination = PKR 5K. 5-star Pearl Continental coordination = PKR 15K.
