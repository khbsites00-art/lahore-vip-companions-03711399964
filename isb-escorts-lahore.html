ISB-Lahore Travel Route Details

The Islamabad-Lahore corridor is one of the most traveled routes in Pakistan, connecting the federal capital with the cultural and commercial heart of Punjab. The distance between the two cities is approximately 375 kilometers, with the M2 Motorway providing a smooth, well-maintained highway that makes the journey comfortable and scenic. Whether you are traveling for business meetings in Lahore or government affairs in Islamabad, having a travel companion makes the journey significantly more enjoyable and productive.

The M2 Motorway, which opened in 2001, was Pakistan's first motorway and remains one of its finest. The four-lane highway features rest areas, fuel stations, and service plazas at regular intervals. The drive typically takes 4 to 5 hours depending on traffic and rest stops. For travelers who prefer not to drive themselves, we can arrange private chauffeur-driven vehicles for the journey, allowing you and your companion to relax, converse, or work during the trip.
