🏏 Iconic Places

Bahria Town's Iconic Landmarks

Bahria Town Lahore is home to some of the most recognizable landmarks in all of Lahore, making it a destination in itself.

🇫🇦

Eiffel Tower Replica

The crown jewel of Bahria Town — a stunning replica of Paris's Eiffel Tower standing at 105 feet, beautifully illuminated at night. The surrounding commercial area is the social hub of the community, featuring restaurants, cafes, and shops. An ideal setting for romantic evenings and memorable dates.

🕌

Bahria Grand Mosque

Inspired by the Blue Mosque of Istanbul, the Grand Mosque is an architectural masterpiece featuring stunning Ottoman-inspired design, towering minarets, and a vast prayer hall. The mosque complex includes beautifully landscaped gardens and serves as a cultural and spiritual center for the community.

🐸

Safari Park

A sprawling wildlife park featuring exotic animals, lush green landscapes, walking trails, and recreational areas. Safari Park offers a unique experience within Bahria Town, complete with a lake, amphitheater, and adventure zones. Perfect for daytime outings and relaxed evenings.

🎬

Carnival Movie Theater

A state-of-the-art multiplex cinema featuring the latest blockbuster films, premium screening rooms, and a full food court. Carnival is the entertainment anchor of Bahria Town, drawing visitors from across Lahore for a premium movie-going experience.
