Lahore's nightclub scene centers around two main areas: Gulberg and DHA.   • Wine & Dine (MM Alam Road): A popular rooftop venue offering panoramic city views, international cuisine, and an extensive cocktail menu. Perfect for pre-club dining and drinks.
