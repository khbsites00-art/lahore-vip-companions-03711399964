Where is Gulberg in Lahore?

Gulberg is one of Lahore's most upscale and centrally located neighborhoods, situated between Mall Road and Ferozepur Road. It is divided into Gulberg I, II, III, and the Main Boulevard area, with MM Alam Road and Liberty Market serving as its commercial and entertainment heart. It's approximately 15 minutes from Lahore Airport and easily accessible from all major city routes.

What restaurants are good for dates in Gulberg?

Gulberg is Lahore's dining capital. Top picks include Forno A Golosina for Italian, Howdy's BBQ for steaks, Butler's Chocolate Cafe for European ambiance, X2 for Pan-Asian cuisine, and The Q for rooftop dining. Our companions know every venue's strengths and can recommend the perfect match for your mood and preferences.

Which hotels in Gulberg do you serve?

We serve all major hotels in Gulberg including Nishat Hotel Gulberg (5-star), Four Points by Sheraton, Luxus Grand Hotel, Best Western, and all other accommodations in the Gulberg I, II, III, and Main Boulevard areas. Our dispatch team knows every hotel's check-in procedures for discreet arrivals.

How fast is dispatch to Gulberg?

Our average dispatch time to any Gulberg location is 25-35 minutes. Because Gulberg is our primary service zone, we maintain a fleet of companions stationed throughout the neighborhood for rapid response. For Main Boulevard and MM Alam Road addresses, delivery can be as fast as 20 minutes.

What's the best time for Gulberg bookings?

The sweet spot for Gulberg bookings is between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM, when the restaurant and lounge scene is at its peak. Weekend evenings (Friday-Saturday) are the busiest and most vibrant. However, we accommodate bookings at any hour — late-night and early-morning requests are always welcome.

Is Gulberg safe for companions?

Absolutely. Gulberg is one of Lahore's safest and most well-lit neighborhoods, with heavy security presence from private security firms and local authorities. The area's upscale nature, well-maintained streets, and surveillance infrastructure make it the safest zone in Lahore for companionship services.

What's near MM Alam Road?

MM Alam Road is surrounded by Liberty Market (5-minute walk), Main Boulevard hotels (2-minute drive), Packages Mall (10 minutes), Fortress Stadium (8 minutes), and the Mall Road commercial strip (10 minutes). The road itself contains over 100 restaurants, cafes, lounges, and entertainment venues within its two-kilometer stretch.

Do you cover Liberty Market area?

Yes, Liberty Market and its surrounding streets are fully within our Gulberg service area. Many of our companions are particularly familiar with Liberty's evening scene, including its street food vendors, shopping stalls, and the iconic Liberty Roundabout. We dispatch to all Liberty Market hotels and residences.
