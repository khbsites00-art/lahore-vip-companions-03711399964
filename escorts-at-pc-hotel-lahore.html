Hotel Knowledge

PC Hotel Lahore — Our Complete Layout Guide

Understanding Pearl Continental inside and out is what sets us apart. Here is what we know about navigating this iconic property.

Floors & Layout

14 Floors of Heritage

PC Hotel spans 14 floors with over 400 rooms and suites. The ground floor houses the main lobby, business center, and reception. Floors 1-3 contain conference rooms, banquet halls, and restaurants. Guest rooms begin on floor 4, with premium suites occupying floors 10-14. The rooftop features a pool and recreation area. Our companions are most familiar with floors 4-14, where privacy and discretion are optimized.

Room Types

Suites & Rooms We Know Best

Standard rooms on floors 4-7 offer solid privacy and value. Executive rooms (floors 8-9) provide upgraded furnishings and enhanced soundproofing. Deluxe rooms (floors 10-12) are our recommended choice for outcall — they feature larger layouts, separate sitting areas, and are located in the quietest section of the hotel. Presidential and Chairman suites (floors 13-14) offer the ultimate in space and discretion with private elevator access.

Lobby & Entrance

Navigating the Lobby

The main lobby is a high-traffic area, especially during business hours (9 AM - 5 PM) and evening dining (7 PM - 10 PM). Our companions learn to navigate it during quieter windows or use the side entrance on the eastern wing. The concierge desk is positioned centrally — our companions move past it naturally, as any guest would. Late-night arrivals (after 11 PM) are typically the most discreet, as lobby activity is minimal.

Parking & Access

Basement Parking & Valet

PC Hotel's basement parking is accessible from The Mall Road entrance and accommodates over 200 vehicles. Valet parking is available 24/7. Our companions can park in the basement level for discreet access to elevators, or be dropped off at the main entrance. For guests arriving by car, the parking structure connects directly to the lobby via an internal staircase, eliminating the need to exit to street level.

Elevator System

Two Elevator Banks

PC Hotel operates two elevator banks: the main bank (4 elevators) serving floors 1-14, and a service bank (2 elevators) used primarily by staff. Our companions use the main bank during off-peak hours and the service bank during busy periods if authorized. The wait time averages 30 seconds during peak hours and under 10 seconds during off-peak. We time our arrivals to avoid peak elevator traffic.

Privacy Protocol

How We Maintain Discretion

PC Hotel staff are trained to be unobtrusive — a quality we leverage. Our companions arrive dressed appropriately for the setting: business casual or evening wear depending on the hour. They carry no identifying materials. They check in as guests or use the visitor registration system. No one at PC will question their presence. We have never had a privacy incident in over 5 years of service at this property.

Questions

Frequently Asked — PC Hotel

Do you know Pearl Continental Hotel well?
Yes. Our team has served PC Hotel Lahore since 2019. We know every floor, room type, elevator route, and service entrance. We navigate the hotel the way a concierge navigates their own lobby — with precision and discretion. Our companions have been to every floor, dined in every restaurant, and utilized every entrance. This institutional knowledge is what allows us to provide seamless service at this property.
How do companions arrive at PC Hotel?
Our companions arrive discreetly via the main lobby or service entrance, depending on your preference. They check in as guests or use the designated visitor protocol. No one at PC Hotel will question their presence. During peak hours, they use the side entrance. During off-peak hours (late night, early morning), the main lobby is equally discreet. We always coordinate arrival timing to avoid situations where multiple companions might cross paths.
Which rooms at PC Hotel are best for outcall?
Executive suites and Deluxe rooms on upper floors offer the most privacy. We recommend floors 8-14 for optimal discretion — these floors have less foot traffic, better soundproofing, and are?? the lobby noise. Presidential and Chairman suites are also available for extended companionship and offer private elevator access. Standard rooms on floors 4-7 are acceptable but we recommend upper floors when privacy is a priority.
How discreet is the service at PC Hotel?
Absolute discretion is our protocol. Companions arrive dressed for a business meeting or dinner reservation. No branded materials, no identifying calls, no digital trail that links to our service. PC Hotel staff are trained to be unobtrusive — and so are we. We use no logos, no uniforms, and no identifiable accessories. Your companion appears as a friend, colleague, or business associate. There is nothing in their appearance or behavior that would suggest otherwise.
Can companions dine at PC Hotel restaurants?
Yes. Our companions are comfortable in fine dining settings. They can join you at PC Hotel's Cascada (continental and Pakistani cuisine), the Chinese restaurant, or any other outlet. They are trained in restaurant etiquette and can order and converse naturally. For the best experience, we recommend making a reservation for two and having your companion meet you at the restaurant entrance. This eliminates any need for the companion to pass through the lobby.
What's the check-in process at PC Hotel?
PC Hotel Lahore offers traditional front-desk check-in. You can register under your own name. For extra discretion, we recommend requesting a room without mentioning companions. Our companion will arrive after you are settled in your room. If you prefer not to check in yourself, we can coordinate a third-party check-in arrangement — contact us for details. PC also offers online check-in for registered members, which bypasses the front desk entirely.
Is parking available at PC Hotel?
Yes. PC Hotel has a large basement parking area and valet service. Our companions can park discreetly or be dropped off at the main entrance. If you're driving, the parking structure is accessible from The Mall road entrance. Valet parking is available 24/7 and costs approximately PKR 500 per visit. The basement parking connects directly to the lobby via an internal staircase, which is useful for avoiding street-level exposure.
Do you serve other Pearl Continental properties?
Yes. We serve all PC properties including PC Bhurban, PC Peshawar, and PC Karachi. However, our Lahore service is the most established with the fastest response times. For outstation bookings, please call in advance so we can arrange the appropriate companion. PC Bhurban bookings typically require 24-hour advance notice. PC Peshawar and Karachi are available with 48-hour advance coordination.
What are your rates for PC Hotel outcall?
Rates depend on the companion selected and duration. Our PC Hotel outcall service starts from PKR 30,000 for 2 hours. Extended bookings and overnight stays are available. All payments are cash on arrival — no advance required. Premium and VIP companions start at PKR 45,000 and PKR 60,000 respectively. Special occasion pricing (Valentine's, New Year's, Eid) may vary by 15-20%. Contact us for a personalized quote based on your specific requirements.
How quickly can a companion arrive at PC Hotel?
Standard arrival is within 15-25 minutes of your confirmed booking. We maintain a roster of companions who are pre-positioned near The Mall area specifically for PC Hotel bookings. VIP express arrival is available for immediate dispatch — companions in this tier can arrive within 8-12 minutes.
