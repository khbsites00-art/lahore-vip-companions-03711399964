When an Agency Is the Better Fit

We built this platform around independents, and we still point clients toward an agency on occasion. The right choice depends on the booking, not on loyalty to a label.

An agency suits you when timing is rigid. A confirmed replacement within the hour is something no single individual can promise. If your flight lands at midnight and the meeting has to happen before two in the morning, a desk with several companions on call beats one person's good intentions.

Groups are another case. A corporate dinner, a staged event, or several guests arriving together usually needs one invoice, one coordinator, and a single point of contact. Independents bill individually, and coordinating four of them yourself becomes a job in its own right.

First-time visitors also lean toward agencies. If you do not know the city, a desk compresses the research. You make one call, describe what you want, and let somebody else match the roster. Locals and returning clients tend to prefer the direct route, because they already know what they like.

Privacy expectations matter too. Some clients want very little written down. A short call, cash on arrival, and no message history suits that need better than a long WhatsApp thread.

For everything else, direct booking usually wins on price, speed, and warmth. Weigh it honestly rather than picking a side out of habit.

  • Turning up with extra people who were never mentioned.
  • Repeated messages after the answer has already been no.
