Hotel Knowledge

PC Hotel Lahore — Our Complete Layout Guide

Understanding Pearl Continental inside and out is what sets us apart. Here is what we know about navigating this iconic property.

Floors & Layout

14 Floors of Heritage

PC Hotel spans 14 floors with over 400 rooms and suites. The ground floor houses the main lobby, business center, and reception. Floors 1-3 contain conference rooms, banquet halls, and restaurants. Guest rooms begin on floor 4, with premium suites occupying floors 10-14. The rooftop features a pool and recreation area. Our companions are most familiar with floors 4-14, where privacy and discretion are optimized.

Room Types

Suites & Rooms We Know Best

Standard rooms on floors 4-7 offer solid privacy and value. Executive rooms (floors 8-9) provide upgraded furnishings and enhanced soundproofing. Deluxe rooms (floors 10-12) are our recommended choice for outcall — they feature larger layouts, separate sitting areas, and are located in the quietest section of the hotel. Presidential and Chairman suites (floors 13-14) offer the ultimate in space and discretion with private elevator access.

Lobby & Entrance

Navigating the Lobby

The main lobby is a high-traffic area, especially during business hours (9 AM - 5 PM) and evening dining (7 PM - 10 PM). Our companions learn to navigate it during quieter windows or use the side entrance on the eastern wing. The concierge desk is positioned centrally — our companions move past it naturally, as any guest would. Late-night arrivals (after 11 PM) are typically the most discreet, as lobby activity is minimal.

Parking & Access

Basement Parking & Valet

PC Hotel's basement parking is accessible from The Mall Road entrance and accommodates over 200 vehicles. Valet parking is available 24/7. Our companions can park in the basement level for discreet access to elevators, or be dropped off at the main entrance. For guests arriving by car, the parking structure connects directly to the lobby via an internal staircase, eliminating the need to exit to street level.

Elevator System

Two Elevator Banks

PC Hotel operates two elevator banks: the main bank (4 elevators) serving floors 1-14, and a service bank (2 elevators) used primarily by staff. Our companions use the main bank during off-peak hours and the service bank during busy periods if authorized. The wait time averages 30 seconds during peak hours and under 10 seconds during off-peak. We time our arrivals to avoid peak elevator traffic.

Privacy Protocol

How We Maintain Discretion

PC Hotel staff are trained to be unobtrusive — a quality we leverage. Our companions arrive dressed appropriately for the setting: business casual or evening wear depending on the hour. They carry no identifying materials. They check in as guests or use the visitor registration system. No one at PC will question their presence. We have never had a privacy incident in over 5 years of service at this property.

The Decision

Room or Lobby: Where You Actually Meet

Both options work. They suit different evenings, and choosing one before the call saves time later.

A lobby meeting takes five minutes of your evening. You come down, you greet her, and you decide together where the night goes. It is the better choice on a first booking, because you see each other before anything else happens. Coffee in the lounge, a table at the restaurant, then upstairs on your own schedule.

A room meeting is faster and quieter. You are already checked in, she comes straight to the floor, and nobody in the building watches the two of you meet. Regular guests pick this after 11 pm, when the corridors are empty and the ground floor has gone still.

Neither option costs more than the other. The rate band does not change because the greeting happens in the lounge instead of at your door.

One honest note. A lobby hello depends on timing. On a Friday at 8 pm, the ground floor belongs to the restaurant crowd. Ask for a later arrival, or start in the room and go down after the rush has passed.

If you would rather visit her instead, the incall option runs on its own band, from PKR 15,000 to PKR 25,000 for two hours, with the same cash-on-arrival rule.

Etiquette

Reception, Lifts and Security: Basic Etiquette

Five-star hotels in Lahore run visitor rules. None of them are difficult. Following them is what keeps an evening calm.

At reception. Visitors normally sign a register and show an ID. That policy applies to everyone who walks in, guest or visitor alike. If you would rather not stand at the desk, come down and walk her in yourself. Guests do it constantly, and staff barely look up.

In the lift. Keep it boring. Hold the door, face the panel, step out on your floor. No lingering on the landing and no loud conversation in the corridor. The same rule applies to the walk back to the lobby at the end of the night.

At security. The entrance may ask for a bag check or a brief glance at an ID. It takes seconds. Answer normally and keep the tone light. Relaxed guests get waved through; nervous guests attract the questions that relaxed guests never hear.

Dress. The hour decides it. Business casual before dinner, something sharper after seven. She arrives looking like someone you already know, which is exactly the point.

Two things we never do. We never ask hotel staff to bend a rule for us, and we never send anyone to a floor they have not been invited to. Both create attention that nobody in the building wants, including you.

One honest downside. Visitor policies change with the season and with the event calendar. Ask at check-in what your hotel allows after 11 pm. A thirty-second question saves an awkward ten minutes later.

Area Context

Club Road, the Mall and the Gulberg Side

Where the hotel sits decides the traffic you hit, the restaurants within reach, and how fast anyone can get to you.

Pearl Continental stands at the eastern end of the Mall, where Club Road runs off toward Aitchison College and the GOR residences. That corner is the useful landmark to remember when you are describing the address to someone on the phone.

West down the Mall takes you past the Zoo and the Governor House toward McLeod Road, the High Court, and the GPO end of the boulevard. Learn From Others

Common Mistakes Guests Make at Five-Star Hotels

Years of bookings at this property have produced the same handful of avoidable problems. Every one of them is fixed by a decision made before the call, not after it.

Booking the cheapest tier for a formal evening. A standard companion is perfectly good company in a suite. She is the wrong choice beside you at a banquet or a corporate dinner, where the conversation has to hold up across a table. Match the tier to the room you booked and to the event you are attending.

Arriving without a plan. No reservation, no timing, no idea what the evening should look like. Two hours disappear while you both decide. Think it through before the call: where you will eat, what time she should arrive, and how long you are staying.

Meeting in the middle of the dinner rush. Friday and Saturday, 7 to 10 pm, the ground floor belongs to the restaurant crowd. If you want a quiet hello, ask for a later arrival or start upstairs and go down afterwards.

Sending someone up before you are checked in. Visitors need a room to visit. Check in first, settle, then make the call. It takes thirty seconds and it prevents the only scenario that hotel staff will actually notice.

Paying an advance. We do not take them, and nobody credible at this level does either. If somebody asks for one, it is not us.

Forgetting cash. Cards do not work for this. Carry the amount in hand before the evening starts, including a little extra for dinner.

Talking about it in public. Corridors carry sound and lifts carry sound. Keep the details in your phone and behind your door, and the evening stays yours.
