Outcall FAQ

What is outcall escort service?

Outcall escort service means our companion comes to your location — whether that's a hotel room, private residence, serviced apartment, or any designated venue in Lahore. Unlike incall (where you visit us), outcall puts you in the comfort of your own space. You choose the location, and we dispatch a companion to you within 20–40 minutes depending on your area. This is Lahore Companions' most popular service, especially for hotel guests and clients who value privacy above all else.

How long does outcall take to arrive?

Dispatch time depends on your area in Lahore. DHA, Gulberg, and Cantonment areas see arrivals in 20–25 minutes. Johar Town, Model Town, and surrounding areas average 25–30 minutes. Outer areas like Bahria Town, Valencia, and Ravi Town take 30–40 minutes. We provide an exact ETA when you book — no guessing. During peak hours (Friday and Saturday nights), add 5–10 minutes to standard times. We always communicate delays proactively.

Do you cover my area in Lahore?

Likely yes. We cover 16+ major areas including DHA (Phases 1-9), Gulberg, Johar Town, Cantonment, Mall Road, Model Town, Gulshan-e-Ravi, Iqbal Town, Faisal Town, Wapda Town, Cavalry Ground, Bahria Town, Valencia Town, Kot Lakhpat, Ravi Town, and the Old City. If your area isn't listed, call us at 0371-1399964 — we serve virtually every corner of Lahore. Our dispatch network ensures no location is too far.

We serve all major hotels in Lahore including Pearl Continental (PC), Serena Hotel, Nishat Hotel (M.M. Our outcall rates start from PKR 25,000 for a 2-hour session in premium areas (DHA, Gulberg, Cantonment). Extended areas like Johar Town, Model Town, and Wapda Town are PKR 30,000. Absolutely. Discretion is the foundation of our service. Our companions arrive in smart casual attire — never revealing or attention-grabbing. Can I book outcall for multiple hours?
